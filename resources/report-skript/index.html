Player Report Skript – A fast and reliable reporting system for Minecraft servers. This Skript allows players to easily report rule-breakers or suspicious activity with a single command. Reports are instantly sent to staff in-game and simultaneously forwarded to a linked Discord channel, ensuring your moderation team never misses an alert. Features: Simple /report command for players. Real-time in-game notifications to all online staff members. Discord integration (via SkCord) to send formatted alerts to your moderation channel. Clear formatting with player names, reasons, last messages, last kills, ping, and timestamps. Keep your community safe by giving players a quick, reliable way to alert staff whether they’re online or offline. Known servers using this or a changed version of this: BoxCorp Brokits.net/brokits.minehut.gg (java x bedrock) MaceLegends/macelegends.minehut.gg
