Punishment GUI Skript – The all-in-one moderation solution for Minecraft servers. Built using Skript, SkQuery, SkBee, and LiteBans, this system provides staff with a sleek, intuitive graphical interface for managing punishments efficiently. It comes fully loaded with a wide range of predefined offenses, each featuring escalating punishment tiers that persist across sessions. Color-coded indicators make it easy to see a player’s offense history at a glance, ensuring fair and consistent enforcement. Vulcan integration takes moderation to the next level by providing alt detection, detailed punishment history, and comprehensive logs, giving staff all the information they need before issuing a punishment. Combined with LiteBans’ powerful banning system, every action is recorded, synchronized, and enforceable across your server network. Do not redistribute this Skript under any circumstances.
