DupeCore & Lifesteal Skript – Ultimate Duplication & PvP System Take your server to the next level with DupeCore, the all-in-one Duplication and Lifesteal system! This Skript is designed for serious Minecraft servers looking to add advanced item duplication mechanics without breaking the game balance, alongside a toggleable PvP lifesteal system for competitive gameplay. Features: Safe Duplication System: Duplicate any item in your hand with /dupe [amount]. Fully compatible with all standard Minecraft items and shulker boxes. Blacklist support prevents overpowered or rare items from being duplicated. Shulker Box Safety: Any shulker box, regardless of color, is checked for blacklisted items to stop hidden exploits. Cooldown Management: Prevents spamming with fully configurable cooldowns via /dupecooldown. Lifesteal PvP: Toggle lifesteal on or off with {@lifesteal-enabled}. Kill another player to gain hearts and subtract them from your target, with a max of 20 hearts. Heart Economy: Players can withdraw hearts as red dye items with /withdraw and redeem them by right-clicking. Hearts cannot be duplicated or abused. Admin Tools: /dupeblacklist to manage restricted items Why choose DupeCore? This Skript is professional, secure, and ready for monetization. Perfect for servers that want a fun but balanced duplication system combined with engaging PvP mechanics. Safe, easy to use, and fully configurable.
