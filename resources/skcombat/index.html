# ⚔️ SKCombat+ — Advanced Combat Management **SKCombat+** is an all-in-one Minecraft combat management Skript designed to make PvP more competitive, fair, and rewarding. Instead of relying on a large combat plugin, SKCombat+ provides the essential systems your server needs in a lightweight and customizable Skript. ## ✨ Features ⚔️ **Combat Tagging** * Automatically tags players when they enter PvP * 30-second combat timer * Live action-bar countdown * Combat resets when players continue fighting 💀 **Combat Logging Protection** * Players who disconnect during combat are punished * Announces combat loggers to the server * Prevents players from escaping fights by simply leaving 🛡️ **Safe Zone Protection** * Prevent combat-tagged players from entering protected regions * Works great for spawn and other PvP-safe areas 🪽 **Combat Restrictions** * Disable Elytra flight during combat * Block Ender Pearls and Chorus Fruit * Built-in Ender Pearl cooldown * Built-in Enchanted Golden Apple cooldown * Configurable blocked commands during combat 🔥 **Killstreak System** * Automatically tracks player killstreaks * Announces killstreak milestones * Configurable milestone intervals * Give temporary potion effects for reaching streaks * Killstreak resets on death 🎁 **Kill Rewards** * Execute custom console commands when a player gets a kill * Configurable reward system * Toggle rewards without editing the entire Skript 📦 **Loot Protection** * Optional protection for dropped PvP loot * Helps prevent unrelated players from immediately stealing drops * Configurable protection duration and radius ☠️ **Custom Death Messages** * Multiple randomized PvP death messages * Can be enabled or disabled at any time * Makes player deaths feel less repetitive 🏹 **PvP Feedback** * Hit confirmation sounds for bow shots * Combat status displayed directly through the action bar * Clear messages when entering or leaving combat restrictions 🔧 **Admin Controls** SKCombat+ includes the `/cl` administration system for managing combat features directly in-game. Administrators can toggle combat bypasses, remove combat tags, manage blocked commands, configure rewards, toggle loot protection, control Elytra/Ender restrictions, toggle custom death messages, reload the system, and more. **Permission:** `cl.admin` ## 🚀 Perfect For SKCombat+ works especially well for: **Survival • SMP • Lifesteal • BoxPvP • Factions • PvP • Economy • Competitive Servers** Take control of your server's PvP with **SKCombat+** — combat tagging, anti-combat logging, killstreaks, restrictions, rewards, loot protection, and more in one system.
